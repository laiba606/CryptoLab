TOPIC 01

Cryptography Fundamentals

Cryptography is the process of protecting information by converting it into a form that unauthorized people cannot understand.

Important Terms

  • Plaintext: Original readable message.
  • Ciphertext: Encrypted, unreadable message.
  • Key: A value used for encryption or decryption.
  • Algorithm: The method used for encryption or decryption.
  • Cryptanalysis: The study of analyzing or breaking cryptographic protection.

Simple Example

Imagine sending a private message to your friend. Cryptography helps protect the message from unauthorized people.

Caesar Cipher Laboratory

Encrypt and decrypt messages using a simple alphabet shift.

🔤 How does it work?

Caesar Cipher shifts each letter of a message by a selected number of positions in the alphabet.

Example

Plaintext: HELLO

Shift Key: 3

Ciphertext: KHOOR
